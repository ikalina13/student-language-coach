Learn English words, one card at a time.

Student Language Coach is a free Windows app for Filipino students in Grades 4–10. Every word comes with how to say it, its meaning in Filipino, and an example you’d actually hear.

Download for Windows Windows 10 or 11 · 64-bit · Free · Works offline

Everything a student needs to practice

Eight topics — School, Family, Food, Animals, Places, Feelings, Actions and Common Words — at three levels.

Basic Words
Flip cards with pronunciation, the Filipino meaning and an example sentence. Study one at a time or browse them all.
Stories
Short stories set in everyday Filipino life, each followed by three questions to check understanding.
Sentence Practice
Not just single words. Sets of ten simple sentences — pick the missing word and see the Filipino translation.
Vocabulary Quiz
Ten questions like “What does happy mean?” Pick, check, and see right away if you got it.
Daily Word
A new English word on the home screen every day.
Mini Dictionary
Search hundreds of common words in English or Filipino.
Progress Tracker
Words learned, stories completed, quizzes taken and practice progress — all in one place.
Bao, XP and streaks
Bao the carabao cheers you on. Earn XP for every right answer and keep a daily streak going.
Printable report
Save a one-page PDF of your progress to print or send to your teacher.

Installing on Windows

  1. Download Click Download for Windows and save Student-Language-Coach-Setup.exe.
  2. Open the file If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This appears because Student Language Coach is a small free app without a paid publisher certificate.
  3. Create your account Student Language Coach opens by itself. Enter your name, a username, a password and your grade.

Your progress stays on your computer

No internet and no sign-up online. Student Language Coach creates its own save files the first time it runs, so it’s ready on any new laptop or PC. Several students can share one computer — each has their own account.

Using another computer? Progress doesn’t move with you, so export your PDF report before switching.
